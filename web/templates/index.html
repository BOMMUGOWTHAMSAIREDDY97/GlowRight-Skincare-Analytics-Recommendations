Responsible AI & Safety Guidelines

  • No Medical Diagnosis: GlowRight is an algorithmic text-matching prototype and never diagnoses eczema, rosacea, cystic acne, or melanoma.
  • No Allergenic Guarantees: Text matching cannot guarantee personal immunological safety. Patch testing is always necessary for consumer safety.
  • Sentiment ≠ Efficacy: Positive customer reviews indicate cosmetic satisfaction and sensorial appeal, not clinical efficacy.
  • Data Privacy: User queries and exclusions are evaluated ephemerally in-memory and are never permanently stored without explicit consent.
  • Open Sourcing: Starter datasets are clearly tagged as synthetic demo data to ensure zero copyright or customer privacy infringement.

Instructions for Integrating Real Public Datasets

To migrate from synthetic benchmark data to real licensed cosmetics records:

  1. Download an open cosmetics dataset, such as Open Beauty Facts (ODbL) or an open Kaggle Sephora reviews corpus.
  2. Map columns to the schema documented in data/README.md.
  3. Place cleaned CSVs in data/products_demo.csv and data/reviews_demo.csv.
  4. Execute python run_pipeline.py to regenerate all models, charts, and evaluations.
